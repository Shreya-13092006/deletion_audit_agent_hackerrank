### Search scope and interpretation

I searched the exact expected resource IDs through readable participant evidence, activity, identity/audit files, and `code/cache` (PDF text, image facts and raw OCR), excluding `sample_output.csv` as an answer source. The identity-less analytics records do occur in evidence; what is absent is a defensible subject link. Activity `SUCCESS` is a claim, not ownership proof or proof of final state. The two security traces above show why searching only for the gold IDs is insufficient: there are other audit resources, but their trace identities resolve to different users.

The account ambiguity is directly checkable: after excluding case_536 based on its non-deletion request text, three authorized requests remain, but the three failed activity rows still have no link field joining a particular account to a particular requester. Equal counts do not establish the individual mapping. A one-to-one/time-nearest heuristic may choose an account, but it cannot justify the gold assignment, and may misattribute a subject in an equivalent collision.
