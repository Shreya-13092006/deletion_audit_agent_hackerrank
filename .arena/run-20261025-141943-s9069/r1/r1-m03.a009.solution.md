# Decision: no compliant, generalizable patch resolves the ten residual mismatches

## Open questions and assumptions

1. Does “improve” require a code change? No. The task permits a rigorous, checkable demonstration when a case cannot be safely fixed from participant-facing evidence. I use that option where the identity link is absent.
2. Can nearby timestamps, shared batches, cohort labels, or ordering identify a record owner? Only when the evidence makes the link unique and scoped to one subject. Mere proximity or a shared workflow does not.
3. May sample_output.csv be used as evidence for a prediction? No. It is used only to identify and compare the listed discrepancies, never to discover ownership or invent a rule.
4. Can evidence after audit_at prove state at the cutoff? No. Evidence captured at or before audit_at may be considered, but eligibility alone does not make null-keyed evidence an identity link.
5. What is the safe response when evidence names a resource but cannot link it to a subject? Do not emit a subject-specific finding or verification step. Leave the mismatch unresolved rather than inventing ownership.

## Three expert reviews and edge-case decision

**Identity and evidence review:** A resource must be linked through an identifier, a scoped relation, or a uniquely attributable workflow. Shared time windows and anonymous cohort membership do not establish ownership.

**Pipeline review:** The planner can create policy verification steps only for discovered, subject-linked resources. An unconditional check for all records of a type would introduce unrelated resources and unsupported evidence.

**Evaluation review:** Hardcoding sample IDs or parsing the expected answer would violate the no-shortcuts contract and risks hidden-case false positives. The appropriate improvement is to preserve the ownership gate and document its limitations. We settle on no prediction-code patch for these ten cases.

## Ranked recommendation

1. Keep the subject-ownership gate; do not emit speculative resource IDs for the ten cases.
2. To make these predictions resolvable, add participant-facing identity links or uniquely scoped workflow metadata for the resources, then regenerate public answers consistently.
3. Add a general regression invariant: every emitted subject-specific resource step must have a traceable identifier, scoped relation, or uniquely attributable workflow. Keep the existing 22 exact sequences as the no-regression benchmark.
