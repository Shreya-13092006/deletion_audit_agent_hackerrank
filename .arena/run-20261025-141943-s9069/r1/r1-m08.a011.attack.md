ATTACK 1 [MINOR] case_536 is incorrectly counted as a deletion subject
Where: In the case_23 audit: “There are three candidate accounts for four subjects, with no identity evidence to break the allocation.”
Problem: The participant-facing request row for case_536 (`dataset/deletion_requests.csv`) says “I can't access my profile and need help resetting the login. Do not close the account.” Under §6.5 this is not a deletion request, so it cannot be one of the competing subjects. There are three deletion-authorized requests, not four. The stated count-based ambiguity is therefore wrong, although the remaining three requests still lack a supported mapping to the three failed account events.
