## Proposed change and predicted effect

**Unified diff: none.** A code change that emits any of these identifiers would encode a sample answer or use an unsupported inference, violating the no-shortcuts and evidence-linking rules. No generalizable repair is available for these ten cases from the supplied evidence.

Predicted sample effect of retaining the implementation: all 32 statuses stay correct; the same 22 step sequences remain exact; the ten listed sequences remain mismatched. Thus there is no expected regression among the 22 exact matches, and no defensible gain on these ten. The rule also avoids injecting unsupported identifiers into the 518-case run.

## Three approaches considered

1. Infer missing IDs from sample numbering, sequence, or neighboring IDs. Rejected: it memorizes the gold convention and invents evidence.
2. Assign unidentified records by time, batch order, metrics, or elimination. Rejected: the cases share workflow windows and have no subject key; correlations do not prove ownership.
3. Keep the current evidence-backed output and document the irreducible gaps. Chosen: it satisfies the contract and protects hidden cases from guesses.
