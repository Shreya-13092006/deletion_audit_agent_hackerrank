## Why the earlier answer fell short

The earlier answer reached the right no-guessing conclusion, but mostly asserted exhaustive negative searches without giving a reproducible check for these particular IDs, and it did not give a predicted effect against the 32-case baseline. The checks above distinguish three different evidence failures: identifier absent from participant inputs (04/18/20), anonymous resource with non-unique workflow association (03/21/25–28), and competing account deletions (23). That distinction matters: evidence that a resource exists or was anonymized does not prove it belongs to a specific user.

## Ranked recommendations

1. **Keep prediction behavior unchanged until provenance exists.** This is the only defensible immediate action under the evidence and no-case-shortcut constraints.
2. **If the challenge owner wants these cases resolvable, add participant-facing provenance at the source.** For each account delete, include a stable subject reference or request/workflow ID; for retained security records, include the resource ID and subject reference in a permitted ledger; for anonymizations, provide a privacy-safe deletion-job link from the analytics resource to the request. Then use general linkage logic and test both these samples and the full regression set.
3. **Keep an explicit tie/unknown outcome in internal diagnostics.** Do not turn the heuristic `reported_at` window or row order into ownership evidence. Revisit the cases only when a new participant-visible link can distinguish candidates.

## Assumptions

- The supplied sample answers identify the ten scoring diffs but are not permissible evidence for assigning hidden or public resources to people.
- A workflow timestamp or an anonymous analytics row is not a unique identity link unless other participant-facing evidence establishes it.
- “Improve” means improve defensibly under the stated generalization and evidence constraints; exact-match gains achieved through case-specific identifiers or arbitrary tie-breaking do not qualify.
